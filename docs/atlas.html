Atlas

Every level Kenya is divided into, on one map. Hover to name a county, click to go down a level. The two tabs are two different countries administratively: the devolved units in force today, and the provincial administration they replaced.

Drawn from the same data the package ships. Geometry is data/svg/counties.svg and data/county-outlines.json, simplified to roughly a kilometre. Finer boundary tiers live in kenya-regions-geo.
